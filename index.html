Profile

Biography

Mr. Kamil one of the founding partners of IKMW Law.

Mr. Kamil has approximately eight years of litigation experience spanning insurance defense, premises liability, construction defect, commercial litigation, and general civil disputes. Before starting IKMW Law, he represented international conglomerates, national insurers, businesses, and individuals in high-exposure cases involving construction defect, products liability, and personal injury. He has also served as counsel to companies in diverse industries—including a large franchise hair salon group, a motorsport company, and high-profile clients in the e-sports and entertainment industries—advising on compliance, contracts, and risk management. His background includes trial work as well as contributions to appellate matters, including review of an amicus curiae filing before the United States Supreme Court and briefing before the California Court of Appeal.

Mr. Kamil believes effective representation requires collaboration and strategic foresight. He is committed to creative problem-solving, thorough case analysis, and cost-efficient resolution strategies tailored to client goals.
